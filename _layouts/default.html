I am currently working on robotics in stealth. Previously, I was a research scientist at the Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence (Ai2) on the Robotics team under Dieter Fox, where I worked on massively scaling robotics in simulation to enable broadly capable zero-shot generalist policies. Before that, I earned my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Washington, where I was advised by Santosh Devasia and Joseph Garbini.
