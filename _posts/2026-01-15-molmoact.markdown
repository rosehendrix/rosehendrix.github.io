MolmoAct encodes observations into depth-aware perception tokens, generates spatial plans as trajectory traces, and predicts precise actions, achieving 70.5% on SimplerEnv and 86.6% on LIBERO with explainable and steerable behavior.
<br>
<strong>🏆 Best Paper Runner-up</strong> at the <a href="https://rational-robots.github.io/">Rational Robots</a> workshop and <strong>Best Paper Nominee</strong> at the Data workshop at CoRL 2025.
