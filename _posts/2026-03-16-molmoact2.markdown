MolmoAct2 is a fully open action reasoning model built for real-world deployment, with an embodied-reasoning VLM backbone (MolmoER), a flow-matching action expert, and adaptive-depth reasoning at a fraction of prior latency. It outperforms strong baselines including π₀.₅ across 7 simulation and real-world benchmarks, and releases the largest open bimanual dataset to date.
<br>
<strong>🏆 Best Paper Award</strong> at the <a href="https://actiongapworkshop.github.io/">TAG</a> workshop at CoRL 2026.
